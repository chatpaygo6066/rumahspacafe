About us

Miri's first spa-café experience

Rumah Spa & Cafe was born from a simple idea: that beauty and comfort belong together. Tucked inside Marina Square, our space invites you to slow down — enjoy a facial, then linger over specialty coffee and a sweet bite, all in a single elegant visit.

Every detail — from the soft palette to the curated menu — is designed to feel like a quiet getaway in the heart of the city.
